Recommendations & Next Steps
  • 1

    Scale Up Test Problems

    Run E2E benchmark with M=14-15, k=5 on real quantum hardware (IBM, IonQ). The QAOA advantage likely emerges at larger problem sizes where greedy degrades.

  • 2

    Hybrid Kernel Pipeline

    Replace RBF kernel with quantum kernel (IQP/ZZ) for downstream 1-NN. This adds a second quantum component that may show advantage on specific data distributions.

  • 3

    Grover 1-NN for Large Training Sets

    Implement quantum nearest-neighbor search. For N_train > 1000, Grover's sqrt(N) speedup in distance comparisons becomes significant.

  • 4

    Warm-Start QAOA

    Initialize (γ, β) from a Trotterized adiabatic schedule (Egger et al. 2021). This can reduce iterations and improve solution quality.

  • 5

    Real Hardware Validation

    Run Grover amplification on actual quantum hardware. NISQ noise will degrade performance but the sqrt(N!/M) oracle-query advantage is hardware-independent.
